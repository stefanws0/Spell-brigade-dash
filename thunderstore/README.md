# Dash

Adds a short dash to The Spell Brigade. Press Space, or LB on a controller.

You can't be hit while dashing. Each dash costs 5% of your max HP, and you start with one charge that refills in
15 seconds. The charge bar sits under your wizard.

Runs start without the dash. Pick up the Dash card in the level-up shop first; after that its upgrades can show up:

- +1 Dash Charge (Legendary): one more charge, up to 2 times
- Quick Recovery (Epic): charges refill 25% faster, up to 3 times
- Long Stride (Epic): dash 30% farther, up to 2 times
- Blazing Dash (Legendary): leaves a trail of fire that burns enemies
- Ethereal Step (Legendary): dashing no longer costs HP

Your dash stats are in the game's stats list (hold Shift).

## Multiplayer

Everyone in the lobby should have the mod. I haven't tested co-op much yet, so let me know if something's off.

## Settings

Keybinds, distance, charges, HP cost and the look of the bar are in
`BepInEx/config/com.stefanws0.spellbrigadedash.cfg`, which is created on first launch.

If the game crashes with the mod, set `SafeMode = true` under `[Debug]` and open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/stefanws0/Spell-brigade-dash).
